02 — Featured Research Paper

Target: IEEE TMI / MICCAI / Nature Scientific Reports

Med-GReF: Medical Grounded Reasoning & Fusion Framework for Hallucination-Reduced VLMs

Safwan Ali, Ms. Sumra Salim — Salim Habib University (2026)

Abstract & Architecture

Vision-Language Models (VLMs) suffer from visual hallucinations and ungrounded assertions in clinical diagnostics. Med-GReF introduces a multimodal cross-attention fusion mechanism that unites vision embeddings (BiomedCLIP), radiomics texture extractions, and XAI feature maps with an NLI Hallucination Verifier & Gate. The verifier assesses visual groundedness to override ungrounded diagnostic claims before inference outputs are returned.

Empirical Results (3-Seed Mean ± Std)

91.2% Diagnostic Accuracy (81.7% → 91.2%)
2.4× Cut Undetected Hallucinations (16.6% → 7.0%)
0.881 ROC-AUC Score (0.570 → 0.881)
p < 0.001 Statistical Significance (Cohen's d = 4.75)
