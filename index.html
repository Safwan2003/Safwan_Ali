Med-GReF: Evidence-Guided Multimodal Fusion & Hallucination Verification for Medical Vision-Language Reasoning

Vision-language models applied to medical imaging often produce fluent claims that aren't backed by the evidence in front of them — a failure mode with direct patient-safety consequences. Med-GReF pairs a frozen vision-language backbone with quantitative radiomics, Grad-CAM visual explanations, an evidence-guided cross-attention fusion network, and a dedicated natural-language-inference verifier that scores whether a stated conclusion is actually entailed by the retrieved evidence.

On a held-out, article-grouped test split of a PubMed-Central women's-health imaging corpus (every ablation row reported as mean ± SD over 3 seeds), the full configuration lifts accuracy from 0.817 to 0.912 and ROC-AUC from near-chance (0.570) to 0.881, and cuts undetected evidence/conclusion contradictions by roughly 2.4× (16.6% → 7.0%) — catching ~6× as many hallucinations as a confidence-threshold baseline. Negative and inconclusive findings (no calibration gain, an earlier single-seed trade-off, silent evaluation-split bugs) are reported rather than dropped. Full data-fetching, vetting, and training pipeline released.

PyTorch · BiomedCLIP · PubMedBERT · MedGemma-4B (LoRA) · cross-attention fusion · NLI verifier · radiomics · Grad-CAM

Featured research · Final-year thesis

AI-Driven Lunar Soil Composition Analysis from Imagery and LLMs

An end-to-end pipeline developed in alignment with SUPARCO's ICUBE-Qamar lunar mission. It analyzes 457 real Chang'e 3 Yutu rover images to estimate six lunar mineral oxides (FeO, TiO₂, MgO, SiO₂, Al₂O₃, CaO), calibrated to Chang'e 3 APXS ground-truth data. The system combines SAM 2.1 for terrain segmentation, a ResNet-18 classifier for geological units, Gemini 2.0 Flash for automated report generation, and a Streamlit "Mission Control" dashboard that enforces physics-based geochemical constraints (oxide-sum conservation).

Python · SAM 2.1 · ResNet-18 · Gemini 2.0 Flash · Streamlit · Remote sensing
