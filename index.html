01About

Engineering AI for production, with a research habit.

I work as an AI Engineer for a digital-solutions agency, where a three-month internship converted into an ongoing engineering role. I work across the full lifecycle — LLM chatbots and RAG pipelines, real-time voice agents with streaming STT/TTS and barge-in handling, agentic multi-agent workflows with AutoGen and AgentScope, and generative-media engines served on GPU infrastructure.
